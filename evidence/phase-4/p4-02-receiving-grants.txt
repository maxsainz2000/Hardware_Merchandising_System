3. What is deliberately not granted, and why (from the file's own header)
===============================================================================

DELETE, on any of the four, to anyone - spec section 12: "Transactional
records are never physically deleted."

UPDATE on receipts, receiptlines and purchasereturnlines - nothing in this
phase's cards edits a receipt, a receipt line, or a return line after it is
written. If a later card needs one, that grant is argued in a new numbered
file, not added here by assumption.
