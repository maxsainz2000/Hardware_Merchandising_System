===============================================================================
3. What is deliberately not granted, and why (from the file's own header)
===============================================================================

DELETE, on any of the four, to anyone - spec section 12: "Transactional
records are never physically deleted."

UPDATE on sales, salelines and salepayments - spec section 10.3: "Completed
sales are never edited or deleted." Nothing in this phase's cards edits a
sale, a sale line, or a payment record after it is written - if a later
card needs one, that grant is argued in a new numbered file, not added here
by assumption, the same rule 0011_receiving-grants.sql's header states.
P5-10 (immutability) is this file's other named consumer, but only to prove
the grant holds, not to widen it - see p5-02-pos-schema.txt's design-
decisions note on why Sales has no persisted in-progress row to update in
the first place.
