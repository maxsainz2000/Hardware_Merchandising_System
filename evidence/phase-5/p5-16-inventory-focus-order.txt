WHY THIS FILE EXISTS.

docs/ui-specification.md section 4 states that live Tab traversal "is
confirmed once by hand per client and recorded in that client's own evidence
file." At the Phase 5 exit review that was true of exactly one client.
Procurement had evidence/phase-3/p3-07-focus-order.txt; the POS citation
pointed at a section about a stale Windows Service; and Inventory - this
client - had no such capture at all. P4-12's own keyboard box cites
InventoryLayoutTests, which asserts the authored TabIndex numbers and
deliberately shows no window, so it cannot speak to what a person pressing
Tab actually gets.

This file is the missing Inventory capture. It is not a Phase 5 deliverable
in itself - Inventory closed at the Phase 4 gate - but the document written
at P5-14 makes a claim across all three clients, and a claim is either true
of all three or it is reworded. Capturing it was cheaper than narrowing the
sentence, and leaves the stronger result.

===============================================================================
1. WHAT WAS RUN
===============================================================================

Identical harness and method to evidence/phase-5/p5-16-pos-focus-order.txt
section 1, against Merchandising.Inventory.MainWindow with a MainViewModel
signed in as inventoryclerk through a stubbed transport - the same fixture
InventoryLayoutTests uses. Window at the origin, sized to the 1366x768 @ 125%
work area (1092.8 x 576.0 DIP), shown and activated; then per tab, focus the
lowest TabIndex and call

  MoveFocus(New TraversalRequest(FocusNavigationDirection.Next))

until focus returns to a control already visited, reconciling every authored
control against the walk. The harness is not committed, for the reason that
file's section 1 gives.

3. WHAT THIS PROVES, AND WHAT IT DOES NOT
===============================================================================

PROVEN HERE:
  - The window fits the work area, shown, at 1093.0 x 576.0 DIP - the same
    figure Procurement and POS record, which is the point of section 3's
    "all three clients declare the same geometry" table.
  - Every control that receives focus is inside the window bounds; no line
    reads OFF SCREEN.
  - Focus visits controls in ascending TabIndex order on all five screens,
    Sign out (99) last before the cycle wraps to the tab strip.
  - Every authored control is either visited or skipped for a named, correct
    WPF reason. No unexplained skip.

NOT PROVEN HERE: the same three limits POS's section 3 lists - rendering
fidelity at real 125% DPI, focus visuals, and traversal with grids populated
and commands enabled. Phase 7's UI pass owns the first two; the third is
DataGrid's own behaviour rather than this window's authoring.

A note on the unnamed text boxes above. Inventory's XAML gives x:Name to
fewer controls than POS's does, so this transcript reads "TextBox <unnamed>
(TabIndex 21)" where POS reads "TextBox CartQuantityBox (TabIndex 23)". The
TabIndex is what identifies the control either way and the walk is unaffected;
it is recorded here because a reader comparing the two files will notice, and
because naming them is a small readability improvement Phase 7's UI pass
could fold in - not a defect, and no card is raised for it.
